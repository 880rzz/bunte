Wiener Lernhilfe – LernMinis

Target group: pupils attending a Vienna primary school. Fee: free. 2026/27 start: 19 October 2026; registration opens 5 October 2026. Subjects: German, mathematics and Deutsch Start, taught in a playful, regular support format.

Locations: several VHS centres across Vienna; the exact course and time are selected during booking. Contact: VHS education hotline +43 1 893 00 83.

Wiener KinderStimmen / Singschule Wien

Target group: Grades 1–4 of primary school. Duration: 50 minutes per week. Locations: 47 sites across Vienna. Content: age-appropriate voice training, movement and dance songs, Austrian and international repertoire, classical and popular children’s music, and basic musicianship.

Additional option: Wiener ABChöre from Grade 3 up to age 12, 75 minutes per week; in 2026/27 in districts 2, 8, 11, 12, 19 and 22. Contact: post-singschule@ma13.wien.gv.at · 01 4000-84426.

Vienna Music Schools – instruments, singing and ensembles

Target group: children and young people up to age 25. Offer: more than 40 instruments, singing, elementary music, orchestras, ensembles, popular music, world music, inclusive music-making, composition and music production.

Time/location: depends on the chosen subject and music-school site; admission is subject to availability and a personal consultation. Non-binding online pre-registration is available at any time.

Dance – Vienna Music Schools

Target group: Creative Children’s Dance from age 4, ballet from age 6; depending on location, jazz, hip-hop and other dance styles are also offered. Frequency: 1–3 sessions per week, up to 150 minutes.

2026/27 locations: Leopoldstadt – Bildungscampus Christine Nöstlinger, Taborstraße 120 / Schweidlgasse 33; Landstraße – Campus Aron Menczer, Otto-Preminger-Straße 1; Donaustadt – Natorpgasse 1; Liesing – Josef-Kutscha-Gasse 1A; inclusive dance/theatre also at Erlaaerstraße 74.

City of Vienna pools – group swimming lessons

Age: beginner courses for children aged 6–13; summer intensive courses for ages 6–17. Format: 10 sessions of about 45 minutes. Fee: €130, including admission on course days; for children’s courses, admission for one accompanying adult is also included.

Dates/locations: offered in autumn, spring and summer; the exact pool and course time are selected in the online booking system.
