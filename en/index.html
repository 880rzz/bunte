Autumn break

Wiener Herbst Camps 2026

Modular, interdisciplinary holiday care with leisure activities as part of Ferien in Wien.

Dates

27 October to 2 November 2026. According to the handout, there is no care on Saturday or Sunday and booking is only possible for the full camp period.

Age groups

Leisure programme: ages 6–12. Inclusive programme: ages 6–12 with increased support needs. Small-group programme: ages 6–14 with increased support needs.

Daily schedule

Modular care 08:00–17:00, core activity programme 09:00–16:00. Additional early care from 07:15 and late care until 17:30.

Fee and cancellation

€80 per child/week including lunch and snack. Free cancellation within 48 hours after booking; after that, cancellation up to and including 12 October 2026 carries a €20 processing fee per child. From 13 October there is no refund. Booked places are not transferable.

Booking: According to the school handout, eligible families can book from 14 September 2026 using access details provided separately by the school; from 28 September booking also opens to an additional eligible group. Booking closes on 8 October 2026. Personal booking codes are intentionally not published on this website.
