Autumn break

Wiener Herbst Camps 2026

Holiday care available through Ferien in Wien. Information from the school-provided handout is kept separate from independently verified public alternatives.

Dates

27 October to 2 November 2026. According to the handout, there is no care on Saturday or Sunday and booking is only possible for the full camp period.

Age groups

Leisure programme: ages 6–12. Inclusive programme: ages 6–12 with increased support needs. Small-group programme: ages 6–14 with increased support needs.

Daily schedule

Modular care 08:00–17:00, core activity programme 09:00–16:00. Additional early care from 07:15 and late care until 17:30.

Fee and cancellation

€80 per child/week including lunch and snack. Free cancellation within 48 hours after booking; after that, cancellation up to and including 12 October 2026 carries a €20 processing fee per child. From 13 October there is no refund. Booked places are not transferable.

Booking: According to the school handout, eligible families can book from 14 September 2026 using access details provided separately by the school; from 28 September booking also opens to an additional eligible group. Booking closes on 8 October 2026. Personal booking codes are not published on this website.

Ferien in Wien – official website
Age-based guidance

What tends to suit each age group?

Ages 6–7

A nearby, full-day, varied leisure programme is usually the best starting point. At this age, a predictable routine, movement and creative play generally matter more than a narrowly specialised theme.

Ages 8–9

A mix of sport, creative work, rule-based games and simple projects can work well.

Ages 10–12

More strongly themed sport, technology, media or creative programmes may fit well and can include more independent tasks.

Ages 6–14 · increased support needs

The inclusive or small-group option may be more appropriate; the exact support level should be confirmed directly with the organiser before booking.

Other autumn camps in 2026

These are not part of Wiener Herbst Camps.

Activity4u Herbstcamp

Age: 6–14. Dates: 27–30 October 2026. Location: Tennispoint Vienna, Baumgasse 87, 1030 Vienna. Fee: €245 / 4 days; €15 sibling discount. Activities: tennis, badminton, chess, squash and table tennis. Arrival 08:00–09:00, activities from 09:00, collection 16:00–17:00.

Recommendation: available from age 6, especially suitable for children who already cope well with a structured, sport-heavy full day.

Official details and registration

SK Rapid Herbstcamp 2026

Age: 7–14. Dates: 26–30 October 2026, daily 08:00–16:30. Location: Allianz Stadion training grounds. Fee: €349; €296 for Greenie members. Professional football training, lunch and snacks, stadium tour, training gear and club activities.

Recommendation: best suited to children aged 7–14 who already play football regularly or are strongly interested in the sport.
