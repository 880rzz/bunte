## Link policy
Dynamic catalogues are not copied as frozen event lists when their dates/availability change continuously. The app links to the authoritative live calendar or booking page. A crawler/network refusal is not treated as proof that a link is dead; only an official replacement or confirmed 404/410 is treated as a broken link.

## Remaining caveats
This is not a literal mirror of every single event currently present in all City of Vienna/WIENXTRA live calendars. It is the verified directory of recurring official programme systems relevant to primary-school children, with live links for event-level availability. School-source inconsistencies are intentionally displayed as such until the school/provider issues updated source material.
